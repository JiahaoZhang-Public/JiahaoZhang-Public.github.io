Transcript

Meet ComputeDeck. See your clusters and cloud GPUs in one place. Open a queued job to understand the wait. Then choose your GPU needs and compare capacity, permissions, and time limits. Read-only by default, with your existing SSH setup. This walkthrough uses example data.

Jobs and capacity

Inspect a queued job

Open a job to see its GPU request, account, QoS and the scheduler’s pending reason.

Compare GPU options

Enter a GPU model, count and run time. Compare visible capacity and verified QoS limits.

Estimates use the last collection. Reservations and priorities can still delay a job.
