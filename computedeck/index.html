Meet ComputeDeck. See your clusters and cloud GPUs in one place. Open a queued job to understand the wait. Then choose your GPU needs and compare capacity, permissions, and time limits. Read-only by default, with your existing SSH setup. This walkthrough uses example data.

BUILT AROUND THE QUESTIONS YOU ASK

More understanding.

A view of the resources available to you,
without a pile of terminal tabs.

01 / FOLLOW YOUR WORK

What’s running.
What’s waiting. Why.

See running, queued and recently failed jobs across cluster logins. Open a job for its requested GPUs, account, QoS and the scheduler’s pending reason.

The same job seen through two logins is counted once.

02 / PLAN THE NEXT RUN

Match your next job
to visible capacity.

Choose a GPU model, a GPU count and a run time. Compare collected node capacity, verified QoS access and limits across your cluster accounts.

An estimate from the last collection. Scheduler priorities and reservations can still delay a job.
